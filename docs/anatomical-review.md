# Anatomical review checklist

Every entry must answer:

- Is the structure real and named correctly?
- Is its category and system assignment defensible?
- Is laterality modeled without confusing a shared outline for a shared object?
- Does the hierarchy make sense for the human display scope?
- Does the simplified outline preserve recognizable morphology at 16–64 px?
- Is provenance recorded in `ontology/sources.yaml` and the anatomy entry?

Every current vertical-slice entry has `review_required: true`. That is an
explicit QA flag for the simplified artwork and classification; it is not a
claim that the object is fictional or unusable.

The 0.1.0 outlines are original atlas-style simplifications, not diagnostic
illustrations. The registry is intentionally narrow and leaves individual
carpals, metacarpals, tarsals, phalanges, ribs, and vertebral levels for expert
review rather than inventing identities.
